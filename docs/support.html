Support & Quick Start

Everything you need to connect your iPhone or iPad with your Mac.

Quick Setup in 3 Steps

  1. Open NidoBridge Host on your Mac menu bar to display your pairing QR code or 6-character code.
  2. Open NidoBridge on your iPhone or iPad and tap Scan QR (or enter the 6-character code).
  3. Your active workspace sessions will appear immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I disconnect or switch to another iPhone/iPad?

Click the NidoBridge icon in your Mac menu bar and select Revoke & Disconnect, or tap Disconnect inside the iOS Controls sheet. Only one mobile device can remain paired at a time.

How do I restore my NidoBridge Pro purchase?

Open NidoBridge on your iPhone or iPad, open the Pro membership sheet, and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the screen.

Why does the app ask for Microphone or Camera access?

Camera access is only used to scan the pairing QR code on your Mac. Microphone and Speech Recognition access are only used when you tap the voice button to dictate a command. Both are optional.

Need additional help?

If you encounter an issue or have feedback, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository or reach out through the support link listed on the App Store product page.
