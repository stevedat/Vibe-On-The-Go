Stay connected to your Mac workspace from anywhere.

NidoBridge is a clean companion app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac. View active sessions, approve pending prompts, and send voice or text instructions to your own computer with a single tap.

Direct 1-to-1 Pairing

Pair your iPhone or iPad with your Mac using a one-time QR code or a short pairing code. Only one mobile device is authorized at a time, and you can disconnect it instantly from the Mac menu bar.

Quick Confirmations & Live Status

See when a task on your Mac is waiting for your confirmation and respond from your iPhone, iPad, Lock Screen Live Activity, or Apple Watch.

Multilingual Voice & Text Input

Dictate instructions naturally in 10 supported languages or use quick action presets tailored to your workflow.
